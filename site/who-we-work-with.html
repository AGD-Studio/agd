02

Property technical sheet

Every property needs a clear, complete and professional presentation.

We create a digital technical sheet for the property, bringing together the essential information about the home in an organized way to make it easier to present and sell.

The technical sheet can be used to

  • Present the home to potential buyers
  • Provide information during viewings
  • Share it by WhatsApp or email
  • Complement your listings
  • Improve the commercial presentation of the property

A well-presented home conveys more information, builds more trust and makes the buyer's decision easier.
